THE EASY WAY

How to order

Choose a drink on the left, type a message, or press the microphone. You can even say “Two large iced oat-milk lattes and a small mocha” to fill several details, and several drinks, at once.

Beanie asks only for missing details. Tap any option, edit your answers, or go back whenever you like. Voice is optional, and spoken input is shown for review before it's used.

When every detail is in, Beanie reads your order back. Say or type “yes” to place it, or tell Beanie what to change. You can also use Review my order. Everything here is a local demonstration with no real checkout or payment.
